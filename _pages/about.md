## About Me


I am looking for a PhD position. I am a biomedical engineering student interested in developing artificial intelligence and data-driven methods for improving healthcare. My work sits at the intersection of AI, biomedical data science, clinical informatics, and cellular analysis, with a focus on translating computational methods into clinically meaningful applications.

I am particularly interested in building reliable, interpretable, and physiologically grounded AI systems that can support clinical decision-making and enable more personalized healthcare.

### Research Interests

- Artificial Intelligence in Medicine
- Biomedical Data Science & Clinical Informatics
- Explainable and Trustworthy AI

### Education

- M.S. in Biomedical Engineering — AI Track
Carnegie Mellon University

- B.S. in Computer Engineering
Chiang Mai University
